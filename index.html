About the Platform

Built for decision-makers who rely on data.

CrimeIQ is an end-to-end analytics platform that helps researchers, analysts, and law enforcement agencies understand crime patterns and anticipate incidents before they happen. From large-scale data exploration to focused prediction workflows, CrimeIQ turns raw data into actionable intelligence.

  • Unified analytics across all crime categories
  • Built-in prediction engine with explainable outputs
  • Real-time alerts, news feed & ticker
  • Designed for desktop, tablet & mobile
